Out the door in three taps.

  1. Open OutRN

    Your picks are already there, fitted to where you are and how long you have.

  2. Pick one

    Each pick shows the cost, the walk and how long you’d get there.

  3. Get directions

    OutRN hands you to your maps app, and you can put the phone away.

Have a budget, or need to be back by a certain time? Add it if you want to. You never have to.

It tells you what it knows and what it’s guessing.

Every detail in OutRN says where it came from. Hours a venue published are shown differently from an estimate, and a pick with something unconfirmed is marked Check first instead of Go now.

When only two things fit your evening, you see two. OutRN won’t stretch your budget or your walk to fill a third slot.
